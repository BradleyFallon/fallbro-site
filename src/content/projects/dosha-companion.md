## What it is

Dosha Companion is a mobile-first product concept combining expert-authored Ayurvedic education, repeatable self-assessment, daily guidance, and a grounded assistant. It is defined as a wellness education tool—not a medical service or a replacement for professional care.

The browser-local prototype includes onboarding, an unscored assessment, deterministic content coverage, repeatable check-ins, provisional recommendations, learning material, search, and local settings.

## Content and safety first

The repository treats product, domain, editorial, safety, data, design, and engineering decisions as separate sources of truth. Content is explicitly provisional or approved, and unresolved domain decisions remain visible rather than being silently filled by software or an AI model.

This structure allows interaction design and content operations to be tested without implying that incomplete scoring rules or unreviewed recommendations are authoritative.

## Current state

The public demo is usable locally and content-driven. Dosha scoring, expert approval, medical claims, and subscription-gated assistant behavior remain intentionally outside the demonstrated feature set.
